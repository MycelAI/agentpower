# AP Ratio Calculator

A simple worksheet for estimating your team's or organisation's agentpower ratio.

---

## Step 1: Define Your Baseline

Before AI, how many knowledge workers did it take to produce your current output?

Think about it this way: if you removed all AI tools tomorrow and had to maintain current output using only human labour and pre-2023 tools, how many people would you need?

```
Current headcount (knowledge workers):     ___________
Estimated headcount needed without AI:     ___________
```

## Step 2: Calculate Your AP Ratio

```
AP Ratio = Estimated headcount needed without AI ÷ Current headcount
```

```
Your AP Ratio:  ___________ ÷ ___________ = ___________
```

### Interpretation

| Your AP Ratio | Where You Stand |
|---|---|
| ~1.0 | You're operating at baseline. No meaningful AI amplification yet. |
| 1.5–2.0 | Early adoption. Some tasks augmented. Room to grow. |
| 2.0–3.0 | Solid integration. Your team is producing well above baseline. |
| 3.0–5.0 | Advanced. You've redesigned workflows around human-AI collaboration. |
| 5.0+ | Exceptional. You're among the early leaders in AI-augmented work. |

---

## Notes

- This is a directional estimate, not a precise measurement. The value is in the thinking it forces, not the decimal places.
- "Cognitive output" is context-dependent. For your team, define it in terms of your actual deliverables: proposals written, cases handled, designs completed, patients processed, reports delivered.
- Quality matters. An AP ratio achieved by producing lower-quality work isn't real amplification. Verify that AI-augmented output meets or exceeds your baseline quality standards.
- Revisit quarterly. Token costs are falling. AI capabilities are improving. Your AP ratio should be increasing over time if you're investing in training and workflow integration.
